# Birbal Sahni

> AI + Robotics project developed during a 21-day practical bootcamp at IIIT-Delhi — **2nd place among 75,000+ students**.

## About

**Birbal Sahni** is an AI + Robotics project developed during an intensive **21-day practical AI and Robotics bootcamp at IIIT-Delhi**.

The project was developed as part of a larger multi-stage selection process that started with **75,000+ students** and progressively filtered participants through multiple rounds of written examinations and selection stages.

The final stage was an intensive **21-day hands-on AI + Robotics bootcamp**, where selected participants worked on practical challenges involving robotics, artificial intelligence, software, hardware, electronics, mechanical design, and system integration.

The project ultimately secured **2nd position** in the final evaluation.
